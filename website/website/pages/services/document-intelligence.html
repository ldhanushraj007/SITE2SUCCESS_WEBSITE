The Challenge & Solution

Businesses spend hundreds of hours having administrative staff manually read invoices, tax filings, bills of lading, and paper forms, and key the numbers into accounting or ERP software. This process is painfully slow, expensive, and riddled with human error. Site2Success builds bespoke intelligent document extraction pipelines: ingest documents via email, WhatsApp, or cloud upload, extract structured data with advanced OCR and vision models, validate GST and math totals automatically, and provide an intuitive split-screen human review console for rapid verification before syncing directly into your database.

What You Get (Deliverables)

  • Multi-source ingestion via WhatsApp, email inbox forwarding, or web upload portal
  • High-precision OCR and AI extraction for printed, scanned, and handwritten documents
  • Deep Indian business document support: GST invoices, bills of entry, challans, KYC
  • Mathematical validation rules (tax rate verification, subtotal + GST summation checks)
  • Side-by-side split screen review interface highlighting low-confidence fields for instant fix
  • Direct integration with Tally, Google Sheets, PostgreSQL, MySQL, and custom ERPs
  • Duplicate detection engine and immutable audit trail tracking who approved each entry

Phased Delivery Plan

Transparent weekly execution milestones with clear deliverables and acceptance criteria.

1 Phase A: Document Discovery (Week 1)

Collect 50–100 sample documents, classify layouts, formats, and scan quality, map exact target fields, and establish accuracy criteria.

2 Phase B: Extraction Pipeline Build (Weeks 2–3)

Build ingestion webhooks, OCR engines, vision-LLM extraction prompts, confidence scoring, and automated math validation rules.

3 Phase C: Human Review Console (Week 3)

Deploy an intuitive split-view web console allowing staff to verify low-confidence extractions in one click, feeding continuous training.

4 Phase D: ERP & Database Integration (Weeks 3–4)

Connect verified extractions directly to Tally, accounting software, or databases with duplicate suppression and audit logs.

5 Phase E: Benchmarking & Team Handover (Week 4)

Evaluate accuracy against unseen document batches, train operational staff, and transfer complete operational documentation.
