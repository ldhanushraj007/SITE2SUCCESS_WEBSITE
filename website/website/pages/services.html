Delivery Operating System

Our End-to-End Client Process

A transparent 9-stage delivery workflow that keeps your project on time, in scope, and under strict quality control.

01

Intake

Client submits initial idea or problem statement. Requirements, urgency, and expected outcomes are recorded.

02

Qualification

Confirm the target user, primary problem, scope boundary, decision-maker, budget range, and delivery timeline.

03

Technical Scoping

Feasibility review; MVP core features distinguished from optional nice-to-haves; timeline estimated accurately.

04

Proposal

A clear proposal is shared outlining agreed scope, milestones, pricing tier, and key architectural assumptions.

05

Kickoff

Single client contact confirmed; shared project channel created; deliverables and acceptance criteria locked in.

06

Build

AI-native rapid development sprints with weekly internal reviews, regular client demos, and clear progress tracking.

07

QA

Comprehensive functional testing, responsive cross-device checks, security review, and mandatory human code audit.

08

Launch & Handover

Production deployment, credentials and repository transfer, full documentation provided, and final acceptance milestone.

09

Aftercare

Feedback collected; testimonial requested; ongoing maintenance or next-phase sprint offered; outcome packaged as case study.
