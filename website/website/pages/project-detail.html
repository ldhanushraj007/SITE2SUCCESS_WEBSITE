Detailed Scope & Architecture

Our client needed an author platform that could serve both as an authoritative public showcase of academic work and as a daily operational workspace for publishing, managing reader inquiries, and cross-promoting content across professional networks.

Site2Success paired an AI-assisted engineering team with senior QA oversight to design, develop, test, and deploy the entire dual-portal architecture in two weeks.

Client-Facing Platform Highlights

  • Structured Academic Showcase: Dedicated modules for About, Experience, Education, Research Projects, Citations, Publications, and Collaborators.
  • Gated Paper Access: User authentication allows visitors to register and log in to request and read restricted pre-prints.
  • Interactive Reader Discussions: Integrated commenting engine under blog articles for direct academic peer review.
  • Direct Inquiry Inbox: Embedded contact system routing reader messages directly to the client's administrative panel.

Autonomous Administrative Portal (Back-Office)

  • Complete CRUD Management: Add, edit, publish, or unpublish publications and research articles in real time without touching code.
  • LinkedIn API Syndication: One-click publishing broadcasts new blog posts and paper updates simultaneously to LinkedIn.
  • Reader Communications Hub: Manage reader comments, review access requests, and respond to incoming inquiries from one screen.
  • Session Security: Role-based access control with secure token generation to protect unpublished manuscripts.
