What is KIRA?

KIRA is an AI engagement platform trained directly on your business data — service menus, FAQs, catalog documents, and qualification criteria. It answers inquiries accurately, captures qualified contact details, and connects directly into your operational workflow.

Because KIRA is tuned with exact parameters and human-verified guardrails, it speaks with brand accuracy without hallucination. Everything from conversation history to real-time analytics lives in a consolidated dashboard.
